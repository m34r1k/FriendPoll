================================================================================
FRIENDPOLL - PROJECT PLAN (draft 6)
Desktop app: invite friends to a poll, play at the times that work
Updated 2026-09-15. Invite-based polls, join requests and a notification
center; demo built. No servers, no channels. (Name: FriendPoll.)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE IDEA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A desktop app for friends to figure out when to play together.

Someone creates a poll: a title, the times they are free, and the friends
they want to play with. Only those friends get the invite. Each invited
friend marks every time they are free as Yes or Maybe. If they are not free,
they just leave it blank - there is no "No".

Every time where enough people said Yes becomes a session. The creator
decides how many people is enough (e.g. 5 for a Valorant 5-stack) and always
counts as one of them, because they already said they are free at every time
they listed. Each poll has its own small chat for just the people in it.

Invited friends can suggest someone else. The creator gets a request and
allows or denies it. A notification center shows what's new across every
poll, so nothing gets lost when lots of polls are running at once.

Example: Ellis creates "MC tonight?" for 9 PM, 11 PM and 1 AM, needs 2
people, and invites Avery, Dana, Casey and Jules.
  Dana: Yes at 9 PM.  Casey: Yes at 11 PM.  Jules: Maybe at 9 PM.
  Avery: hasn't answered.  Dana suggests Micah -> Ellis gets a request.
Result:
  9 PM  - ON  (Ellis, Dana; Jules maybe)
  11 PM - ON  (Ellis, Casey)
  1 AM  - needs 1 more

This is deliberately not Discord: no servers, no channels, no public chat
rooms. The home screen is invites waiting for you and your upcoming sessions.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DECISIONS MADE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Platform     : Desktop app (Windows first)
Desktop shell: Electron (no extra installs needed; Tauri would need Rust)
Backend      : Hosted Supabase, free tier

Structure    : Friends list + polls. No servers, groups or channels.
Friends      : Added by username; the other person accepts (Phase 1).
Creating     : Title, times, one length for all times (1h / 2h / 3h),
               invited friends, and how many people are needed.
Who is in    : Only the creator and invited friends see a poll, its answers
               and its chat.
Adding people: The creator can invite more friends at any time; they're in
               right away. Anyone else in the poll can SUGGEST one of their
               friends: the creator gets a request and taps Allow or Deny.
               The suggested friend hears nothing until it's allowed. The
               person who suggested hears back either way.
Poll size    : Max 20 people per poll, creator included. Enforced by the
               database (Allow is disabled when full).
Answering    : Per time: Yes or Maybe. Blank = not free. No "No". A friend can
               mark any number of times. Tapping your answer again clears it.
Creator      : Automatically in for every time they listed. Does not answer.
People needed: Set by the creator, counts the creator. Minimum 2. Default 2.
Sessions     : A time is ON when (creator + Yes answers) >= people needed.
               Every time is judged on its own - there is no single winner.
Overlap      : Someone who said Yes to several times is in all of them.
Maybe        : Shown, never counts toward people needed.
Visibility   : Everyone in a poll sees everyone's answers.
Ignoring     : No Decline button. An unanswered invite stays in "Invites for
               you" until the poll closes. The creator sees "Not answered yet".
Editing      : Times can't be changed after sending (Phase 5).
Closing      : A poll closes when its last time ends. Answers and join
               requests lock; the chat stays usable; it moves to "Past".

Notifications: A bell with an unread count opens the notification center:
               All / Unread tabs, "Mark all as read", and Allow / Deny right
               on join requests. You get notified when:
                 - someone invites you (and who suggested you, if anyone)
                 - someone asks to add a friend to YOUR poll
                 - the creator allows / denies a friend YOU suggested
                 - someone answers YOUR poll (a changed answer replaces the
                   unread one instead of piling up)
                 - a time you're going to gets enough people (is ON)
               Opening a poll marks its notifications as read. Home shows
               "N new" on polls with unread notifications.
               Chat messages don't create notifications (too noisy); unread
               chat badges come in Phase 4.

Time zones   : Stored as UTC. Each person sees times in their computer's time
               zone automatically, and can override it in settings.
Look         : Light, warm colours. Not Discord's dark grey + blurple.
App name     : FriendPoll (github.com/m34r1k/FriendPoll)
Dev launcher : start-dev.bat - double-click; its window closes with the app
               and only stays open if something failed.

AI features  : (1) Poll from a sentence - "MC this weekend evenings with
               Dana and Jules" fills in the New poll form. The creator checks
               it and presses Send.
               (2) Catch me up - summary of a poll chat you missed.
               (3) "For you" in the notification center - see below.
               AI never decides sessions; that stays plain code.
AI provider  : Google Gemini via Google AI Studio, free tier (no payment).
AI key       : Only in a Supabase Edge Function secret. Never in the app,
               never in .env, never pasted into chat.
AI privacy   : Catch me up and For you send data to Google. Both are OFF by
               default. Catch me up: the poll's creator turns it on per poll.
               For you: each person turns it on for themselves.

"For you" (notification filter):
  Goal: when lots of polls are happening, put what you're likely to care
  about first, based on what you've said Yes to before (which friends,
  which games, which times of day).
  Rules:
    - It sorts and groups; it never deletes or hides notifications.
    - Anything that needs you (a new invite, a join request) is never pushed
      down, whatever the AI thinks.
    - It runs a few times a day at most, not per notification, so the free
      Gemini quota lasts.
  Recommendation: build a plain-code version first (Phase 4) - e.g. "you said
  Yes to 8 of Ellis's last 10 polls", "you never answer before 6 PM". It's
  free, instant, private, and may be good enough. Then Gemini (Phase 3b)
  adds what plain code can't: knowing "MC" and "Minecraft" are the same
  game, and a short reason like "You usually join Ellis's Minecraft nights".

Considered and rejected:
  - Discord-style servers + channels (Phase 0 demo). Replaced by invite-based
    polls: you choose exactly who to play with each time.
  - Self-hosting on the a home NAS. Friends connect over the internet, so
    the NAS (which holds personal files) would have to be reachable from
    outside. Not worth the risk when hosted Supabase is free at this size.

3. STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Desktop shell : Electron
UI            : React 19 + TypeScript
Build tool    : Vite (via electron-vite - handles main/preload/renderer together)
Styling       : Tailwind CSS
State         : Zustand (small, no boilerplate) + Supabase realtime subscriptions
Dates / zones : Built-in Intl API for formatting in any time zone (no library)
Backend       : Supabase
                  - Postgres for data
                  - Supabase Auth for login (email + password to start)
                  - Realtime for live invites, answers, notifications and chat
                  - Row Level Security so people only see polls they are in
                  - Database triggers create notifications, so the app can't
                    fake or skip them
Packaging     : electron-builder -> .exe installer for Windows
AI            : Google Gemini API (AI Studio free tier), called ONLY from a
                Supabase Edge Function. The function checks the caller is
                logged in (and in the poll, for Catch me up), and caps
                requests per user per day so nobody can burn the free quota.
                Replies are requested as structured JSON and validated.

The SQL schema will live in the repo as migration files that you paste into the
Supabase SQL editor, so nothing has to be set up by hand-clicking.

Supabase project settings (chosen at project creation):
  Project ID                      : <your project id>
  Project URL                     : https://<your-project-id>.supabase.co
  Publishable key                 : in .env (verified working 2026-09-14)
  Data API                        : ON  (supabase-js reads/writes through it)
  Automatically expose new tables : OFF (every migration must GRANT access to
                                          the "authenticated" role explicitly,
                                          table by table and function by function)
  Automatic RLS                   : ON  (new tables deny everything until the
                                          migration adds policies)
  Confirm email                   : recommended OFF for now (Authentication ->
                                          Sign In / Providers -> Email). The
                                          built-in email sender only allows a few
                                          emails per hour. Turn it back on with a
                                          custom email (SMTP) provider in Phase 4.
                                          The app handles either setting.

Migrations (supabase/migrations), run in the SQL Editor in order:
  0001_accounts_friends.sql       : RUN on the project 2026-09-15; sign-up and
                                    friends confirmed working by the user
  0002_polls.sql                  : written + tested locally 2026-09-15
                                    (PGlite, 65 checks); NOT YET RUN on the
                                    project

5. DATA MODEL (Supabase / Postgres tables)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every time column is timestamptz, which Postgres stores as UTC. Converting to
the viewer's zone only happens in the app, at display time.

profiles
  id            uuid  PK, references auth.users
  username      text  unique              (how friends find you)
  display_name  text
  avatar_url    text  null
  timezone      text  null                (null = computer's zone)
  created_at    timestamptz

friendships                  (one row per pair of people)
  user_low      uuid  -> profiles         (the smaller of the two ids, so a
  user_high     uuid  -> profiles          pair can only exist once)
  requested_by  uuid  -> profiles
  status        text  ('pending' | 'accepted')
  created_at    timestamptz
  accepted_at   timestamptz null
  PK (user_low, user_high)

polls
  id            uuid  PK
  creator_id    uuid  -> profiles
  title         text                      (1-80 characters)
  min_people    int   default 2           (>= 2, counts the creator)
  closes_at     timestamptz               (end of the last time)
  ai_catch_up_enabled  bool default false (creator-only setting)
  created_at    timestamptz

poll_invitees
  poll_id       uuid  -> polls
  user_id       uuid  -> profiles
  invited_at    timestamptz
  suggested_by  uuid  -> profiles  null   (set when added via a join request)
  PK (poll_id, user_id)

poll_times
  id            uuid  PK
  poll_id       uuid  -> polls
  starts_at     timestamptz
  ends_at       timestamptz               (starts_at + the poll's length)

poll_responses               (one row per person per time they marked)
  time_id       uuid  -> poll_times
  user_id       uuid  -> profiles
  answer        text  ('yes' | 'maybe')   (no 'no' - blank means not free)
  responded_at  timestamptz
  PK (time_id, user_id)

  Clearing an answer deletes the row. The creator never has rows.

join_requests                (an invitee suggesting one of their friends)
  id            uuid  PK
  poll_id       uuid  -> polls
  requested_by  uuid  -> profiles         (must be in the poll)
  user_id       uuid  -> profiles         (the friend to add)
  status        text  ('pending' | 'approved' | 'denied')
  created_at    timestamptz
  decided_at    timestamptz null
  Only one pending request per (poll_id, user_id).

poll_messages                (the poll's chat)
  id            uuid  PK
  poll_id       uuid  -> polls
  author_id     uuid  -> profiles
  content       text
  created_at    timestamptz
  edited_at     timestamptz null
  deleted_at    timestamptz null          (soft delete)

notifications
  id              uuid  PK
  user_id         uuid  -> profiles       (who receives it)
  kind            text  ('invited' | 'join_request' | 'join_approved' |
                         'join_denied' | 'answered' | 'session_on')
  poll_id         uuid  -> polls
  actor_id        uuid  -> profiles       (who caused it)
  subject_id      uuid  -> profiles null  (friend to add / who suggested you)
  time_id         uuid  -> poll_times null
  answer          text  null
  join_request_id uuid  -> join_requests null
  created_at      timestamptz
  read_at         timestamptz null
  Written ONLY by database triggers on poll_invitees, join_requests and
  poll_responses. People can read their own and set read_at; nothing else.

Database functions (the app can't bypass these - RLS blocks direct inserts):
  create_poll(title, start_times, length, invitee_ids, min_people)
    Poll + times + invitees in one step. Refuses if an invitee isn't an
    accepted friend, the poll would exceed 20 people, a time is in the past,
    there are no times or invitees, or min_people is out of range.
  invite_more(poll_id, user_id)           creator only; friend; not full
  request_join(poll_id, user_id)          caller in the poll; user_id is the
                                          caller's friend; not already in;
                                          no pending request for them
  decide_join_request(request_id, allow)  creator only; poll still open;
                                          allow adds the invitee (if not full)

Who can see / do what (Row Level Security):
  "In the poll" = the creator or an invitee.
  - Read a poll, its times, answers, invitees and chat: in the poll.
  - Read join requests: the creator, and the person who made the request.
  - Add / change / delete an answer: invitees only, own row, poll still open.
  - Send a chat message: in the poll.
  - Read notifications / mark read: only your own.
  - Change ai_catch_up_enabled: creator only.

Sessions (computed in the app, not stored):
  going(time) = [creator] + invitees who answered Yes for that time
  ON when count(going) >= min_people
  needed = max(0, min_people - count(going))
  The session_on notification trigger uses the same rule.

Closing (no background job needed):
  A poll is closed when now() > closes_at. The app compares times when drawing;
  RLS enforces the lock on the server.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW LIVE UPDATES WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Supabase Realtime pushes Postgres changes over a websocket. Subscriptions:

  1. notifications where user_id = me
     -> the bell count and notification center update immediately
        (this also covers new invites and join requests)
  2. poll_responses for the polls I'm in
     -> when a friend answers, everyone's times and Upcoming update
  3. poll_invitees / join_requests for the poll I have open
     -> People list and requests update
  4. poll_messages for the poll I have open
     -> chat messages appear instantly

A poll whose last time passes moves to "Past" by itself (the app re-checks the
clock every 30 seconds), so nobody has to refresh.
